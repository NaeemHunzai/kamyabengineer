Help — How to use

Quick guide to main inputs and terms.

  • City / PSH: Pick your city to auto-fill average Peak Sun Hours (PSH). You can fine-tune PSH.
  • Monthly or daily: Enter either monthly bill (USD) or daily kWh; app converts for you.
  • Panel W: Typical panel peak watt rating (e.g., 400 W).
  • Losses: System losses (soiling, wiring, inverter, etc.) — default 20%.
  • Battery hours: Hours of backup required (for hybrid/off-grid).
  • Review: Confirm values on Review screen before viewing Visuals.
